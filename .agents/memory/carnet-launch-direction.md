---
name: CARNET launch direction
description: The approved visual direction for the patient portal's opening experience.
---

Keep the opening experience as an original illustrated healthcare landscape:
sky and clouds, a central clinic, city and rural context, a road and moving car,
with the prominent “CARNET by NaviMED” wordmark.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved this visual direction as excellent after
seeing it rendered at phone size. It captures the welcoming narrative quality
they wanted without copying the reference brand's artwork or logo.

**How to apply:** Preserve this composition and restrained motion when refining
startup visuals. Continue to respect reduced motion, maintain a manual sign-in
shortcut, and bypass the opening screen for restored authenticated sessions.
